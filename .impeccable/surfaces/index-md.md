# Home (index) — and the blog index and article that inherit its world

Scope: `index.md` (layout `default`), `blog.md`, `_posts/*`. Visitor mode: Persuade on the home page; Read on the article.

Audience and job: recruiters scanning for role, years, technologies and a way to reach Brian; developers arriving for a package or an article. Action: write to him. Proof: three roles with employers and periods, two published pub.dev packages, one article. No photo, no certificates, no phone.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is a Clean Architecture diagram of a person. Identity sits at the core and each section is a layer further out, so the structure itself states how he works. It refuses the dev-portfolio default of a dark page with a neon accent and a terminal costume.

OWN-WORLD: One green taken through four depths, one per layer: white core, pale green, mid green, near-black green, on a paper ground. Onest for text, JetBrains Mono for labels and technology names. 1.5px rules, 6px corners, nested rounded squares as the only figure, and a four-ring glyph that marks each section's layer.

STORY: The visitor reads who he is and how long he has done it, sees that the sections are layers of one system, checks where a technology was used, and writes to him.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left, the name at display size, role and city in mono, a one-sentence lead and three actions with "Escribirme" primary. Right, the four nested layers at about 30rem, each a link to its section, with the core naming him. Top bar holds name, section links and a second "Escribirme".

FORM: Clean Architecture layer diagram, seventh on the ordered list. Signature interaction: choosing a skill dims the roles that did not use it and lights the matching technology in the ones that did. Seed key f367c1f6.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
